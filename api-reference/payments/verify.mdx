## Overview

Confirm a payment from your server. `reference` can be a checkout `id`, the `reference` from a `checkout.paid` webhook, or the reference of a payout you sent. A checkout payment returns `checkout_id`, `payment_method`, `paid_amount` (what the payer sent) and `amount` (what you were credited). `400 payment not found` means the checkout is not paid yet or the reference is not yours. See [Verifying a payment](/payments/verification).
