## Overview

Confirm a payment from your server. `reference` can be a checkout `id`, the `reference` from a `checkout.paid` webhook, or the reference of a payout you sent. A checkout payment returns `checkout_id`, `payment_method`, `paid_amount` (what the payer sent) and `amount` (what you were credited). For a payout, `ref` is the same value Send Payment answered as `transaction_ref`; a failed payout reads `cancelled`. `400 payment not found` carries `data.code`: `checkout_unpaid` (your checkout, not paid yet) or `payment_not_found` (nothing of yours under this reference). Not found never means a payout failed. See [Verifying a payment](/payments/verification).
