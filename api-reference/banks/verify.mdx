## Overview

Resolves an account number at a bank to the account holder's name. Show the name to whoever is paying before you send: a wrong `bank_code` or a mistyped account number is refused here, rather than after the money has moved.

`bank_code` must come from [List banks](/api-reference/banks/list) for the same `country_code`.
