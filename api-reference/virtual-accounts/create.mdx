## Overview

Open a bank account your customer can be paid into. Payments into it are credited to the customer and reported on the `account.funded` webhook.

You don't have to create the customer first. Name them with **your own** customer id, send their details, and the customer and the account are opened in one request.

## Naming the customer

Send **one** of these. Sending both answers `400`.

| Field | What it takes |
|---|---|
| `customer_id` | Our customer id, or your own id for the customer |
| `customer_reference` | Your own id for the customer |

We look the value up within your business: our id first, then a customer whose `reference` is that value.

- **Existing customer**: no details are needed.
- **New customer** (no customer has your id yet): send the details below, and the customer is created with your id as their `reference`. Repeating the request finds the same customer; it never creates a second one.
- **Unknown id and no details**: `400` `customer was not found`. An id in our customer-id format that isn't one of your customers is never taken as your own id.

Your ids are 1–64 characters of letters, digits and `_ . : -`, unique within your business.

### Your customer id is the customer

Within your business, a customer is your `customer_id`, not their email, phone or BVN. One person may be several of your customers (for example one per loan), and they may share an email, phone and BVN. A `customer_id` we don't know yet always creates a new customer under it, even when another of your customers has the same email.

- Sending **our** customer id keeps working as before.
- One `customer_id` is one person: if the customer's BVN was verified and the request sends a different `id_number`, the answer is `409` `customer_id_conflict` and nothing is opened. Use a new `customer_id` for a different person.
- For limits and monitoring we still count your customers who share a BVN as one person. That link is never shown in the API.

### New customer details

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| `customer_name`, or `customer_first_name` + `customer_last_name` | Yes. `customer_name` is split at the first space |
| `customer_email` | Yes |
| `customer_phone` | Yes |
| `customer_country_code` | Defaults to `NG` for NGN; required otherwise |
| `customer_date_of_birth` | `YYYY-MM-DD`. Required, **except for NGN when you send `id_number` (BVN)**: the date of birth is then taken from the BVN record |
| `customer_gender` | No. `male` or `female` |

A missing detail answers `422` listing each missing field.

## Currency

- **NGN**: in live, `id_number` (the customer's BVN) is required unless the customer already has a BVN on file. It is verified before the account is opened. See [Choosing the bank](#choosing-the-bank-ngn).
- **USD**: set a default USD settlement wallet in your [dashboard settings](https://business.platnova.com/settings?section=settlements); any account fee is charged to it and returned as `amount_debited`. The customer needs an address and identity documents on file, so create USD customers with [Create Customer](/api-reference/customers/create) first.

## Choosing the bank (NGN)

Send `bank` to choose where the account is opened, or leave it out.

| `bank` | Bank shown to your customer | `bank_code` |
|---|---|---|
| `paga` | Paga | `100002` |
| `nombank` | Nombank MFB | `090645` |

- **Without `bank`** your business's banks are tried in order: **Paga first, then Nombank**. When Paga can't open more accounts (its account limit), the account is opened at Nombank in the same request. Nothing changes in the request or the response shape: read `bank_name` and `bank_code` from the account you get back.
- **With `bank`** only that bank is asked. If it can't open the account you get its refusal, never another bank.
- Both banks give a 10-digit NUBAN, and payments into either are credited the same way and reported on `account.funded` with the account's `bank_name` and `bank_code`.
- `codes` still works. Don't send it with a different `bank`.
- Without `reference`, a customer has one account per bank. Asking without `bank` when the customer already has an account at Paga or Nombank answers `400` `bank account is already added`.

## One account per loan or order

Add `reference` to open an account for one loan, order or contract. A customer can hold several accounts, one per reference, and every payment into an account carries its reference on `account.funded`.

- `reference` is the idempotency key: repeating the request with the same `reference`, customer and currency returns the same account. Nothing new is opened, and nothing is checked against the limit below.
- A new `reference` opens a new account for the same customer.
- A person can hold at most **20** open accounts with your business by default, counted across every `customer_id` that shares their BVN. One more answers `409` `account_limit_reached` with `data.limit` and `data.active`. Contact support to change your limit.
- Look an account up with [Get Virtual Account by Reference](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/by-reference).
- Without `reference`, a customer has one account per bank, and a second request for the same bank answers `400` `bank account is already added`.
- For repayments, a [dynamic account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) per order (with `amount` and `reference`) is often the better fit: it expires, so it never adds to the customer's open accounts, and `account.funded` carries the order `reference` and `expected_amount`.

## Errors

| Status | `data.code` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `duplicate_reference` | The `reference` belongs to another account of your business (another customer or currency) |
| `400` | `invalid_reference` | The `reference` breaks the format |
| `400` | `invalid_bank` | `bank` is not `paga` or `nombank`, or disagrees with `codes` |
| `400` | `bank_not_offered` | Your business doesn't open accounts at that bank. Leave `bank` out, or contact support |
| `400` | — | `customer was not found`; both `customer_id` and `customer_reference` sent; BVN missing, not found or not verified; USD settlement wallet missing or short |
| `409` | `reference_in_progress` | The first request with this `reference` is still opening the account. Retry shortly |
| `409` | `account_limit_reached` | Nothing was opened. Either the bank can't open another account for this customer (`data.accounts` lists their accounts there), or the person already holds your business's limit of open accounts (`data.limit`, `data.active`) |
| `409` | `account_in_progress` | Another request is opening this customer's account right now. Retry shortly |
| `409` | `customer_id_conflict` | The customer's BVN was verified and `id_number` is a different one. `data.customer_id` is the customer. Use a new `customer_id` for a different person |
| `422` | — | New customer: a detail is missing or in the wrong format. `errors` lists each field |
| `422` | `invalid_customer_details` | The bank rejected the customer's details (`data.field` when it named one). Fix them before retrying |
| `503` | `bank_unavailable` | No bank could open the account right now (without `bank`, both Paga and Nombank were asked). Retry later (`data.retryable` is `true`), or use a [dynamic account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) |

See [Creating Accounts](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts) for the full guide.
