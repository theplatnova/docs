## Overview

Open a bank account your customer can be paid into. Payments into it are credited to the customer and reported on the `account.funded` webhook.

You don't have to create the customer first. Name them with **your own** customer id, send their details, and the customer and the account are opened in one request.

## Naming the customer

Send **one** of these. Sending both answers `400`.

| Field | What it takes |
|---|---|
| `customer_id` | Our customer id, or your own id for the customer |
| `customer_reference` | Your own id for the customer |

We look the value up within your business: our id first, then a customer whose `reference` is that value.

- **Existing customer**: no details are needed.
- **New customer** (no customer has your id yet): send the details below, and the customer is created with your id as their `reference`. Repeating the request finds the same customer; it never creates a second one.
- **Unknown id and no details**: `400` `customer was not found`. An id in our customer-id format that isn't one of your customers is never taken as your own id.

Your ids are 1–64 characters of letters, digits and `_ . : -`, unique within your business.

### Your customer id is the customer

Within your business, one person is one customer. A `customer_id` we don't know yet creates a customer under it, unless the person is already your customer (the same BVN, or the same email and phone number): then the request is answered for that existing customer, and nothing new is created. The answer's `customer_id` and `customer_reference` are the existing customer's.

- Sending **our** customer id keeps working as before.
- One `customer_id` is one person: if the customer's BVN was verified and the request sends a different `id_number`, the answer is `409` `customer_id_conflict` and nothing is opened. Use a new `customer_id` for a different person.
- For limits and monitoring we still count your customers who share a BVN as one person. That link is never shown in the API.

### New customer details

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| `customer_name`, or `customer_first_name` + `customer_last_name` | Yes. `customer_name` is split at the first space |
| `customer_email` | Yes |
| `customer_phone` | Yes |
| `customer_country_code` | Defaults to `NG` for NGN; required otherwise |
| `customer_date_of_birth` | `YYYY-MM-DD`. Required, **except for NGN when you send `id_number` (BVN)**: the date of birth is then taken from the BVN record |
| `customer_gender` | No. `male` or `female` |

A missing detail answers `422` listing each missing field.

## Currency

- **NGN**: in live, `id_number` (the customer's BVN) is required unless the customer already has a BVN on file. It is verified before the account is opened. See [The bank](#the-bank-ngn).
- **USD**: set a default USD settlement wallet in your [dashboard settings](https://business.platnova.com/settings?section=settlements); any account fee is charged to it and returned as `amount_debited`. The customer needs an address and identity documents on file, so create USD customers with [Create Customer](/api-reference/customers/create) first.

## The bank (NGN)

NGN customer accounts open at **Nombank MFB** (`bank_code` `090645`) or **Rex Microfinance Bank** (`bank_code` `090449`), each a 10-digit NUBAN.

- **Leave `bank` out** (recommended). The account opens at Nombank. Nombank opens it against the customer's **BVN or NIN**, and holds **at most two accounts per BVN or NIN**, counted across every business we serve. When a customer is already at that limit, the account opens at the next bank we offer your business, in the same request: Rex Microfinance Bank, then **Paga** (`bank_code` `100002`). The response looks the same either way: read `bank_name` and `bank_code`.
- **Send `"bank": "rex"`** to open the account at Rex Microfinance Bank only. If Rex can't open the account, the answer is `503` `bank_unavailable` and nothing is opened.
- **Send `"bank": "nombank"`** to allow Nombank only. At the limit the answer is then `422` `nomba_identity_limit` and nothing is opened.
- Nothing else moves an account to another bank. If Nombank can't be reached, or we can't confirm whether it opened the account, the answer is `503` `issuing_unavailable` with `data.retryable` `true`. No account is given to you then, though one the bank opened may still be confirmed later: retry with the same `reference`, and contact support if it persists.
- At Rex, a retry is safe: repeating the request after a timeout returns the account Rex already opened, never a second one.
- Accounts your customers already hold at PalmPay or Paga keep receiving payments. Each is being given a Nombank replacement (see [Replaced accounts](/virtual-accounts/replaced-accounts)).
- A customer has one open account per bank. Asking again for a bank where they already have one answers that account, with `"existing": true`; nothing is opened or charged. A customer may hold one account at Nombank and one at Rex.
- [Dynamic accounts](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) are not offered at Rex: `"bank": "rex"` there answers `400` `invalid_bank`.

## Choosing a provider

`provider` is optional. **Leave it out and nothing changes**: the account opens at your default for the currency. Send `bridge` for USD, or a naira bank (`nombank`, `paga`, `safehaven`, `rex`, the same names as `bank`).

- A customer holds at most **one account per provider per currency**; accounts at two different providers in one currency are allowed.
- Only providers your business offers in that currency are accepted; any other answers `400` `bank_not_offered`. `nuvion` does not open customer accounts yet and answers the same.
- `provider` and `bank` name the same choice. Send both only when they name the same bank; otherwise `400` `invalid_bank`.
- Each entry in `accounts` names its `provider` and its `status`: `active`, or `pending` while the bank reviews the customer. A pending account has no number yet. You receive `account.pending`, then `account.issued` with the details or `account.rejected` with the reason (see [Pending accounts](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts#pending-accounts)).

## One account per loan or order

Add `reference` to name the account by your own reference. A customer holds **one open account per bank**, whatever the reference:

- `reference` is the idempotency key: repeating the request with the same `reference`, customer and currency returns the same account.
- A new `reference` for a bank where the customer already has an account answers that account (`"existing": true`, with the `reference` it was opened under). Nothing is opened, and the new reference is not kept.
- If the same person holds an account at that bank under another of your customer ids, the answer is `409` `customer_has_account_at_bank`, with `data.customer_id` and `data.account`. Use that customer and its account.
- Look an account up with [Get Virtual Account by Reference](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/by-reference).
- For repayments, a [dynamic account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) per order (with `amount` and `reference`) is often the better fit: it expires, so it never adds to the customer's open accounts, and `account.funded` carries the order `reference` and `expected_amount`.

## Errors

| Status | `data.code` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `duplicate_reference` | The `reference` belongs to another account of your business (another customer or currency) |
| `400` | `invalid_reference` | The `reference` breaks the format |
| `400` | `invalid_bank` | `bank` or `provider` is not a name we know, `provider` and `bank` name different banks, or `bank` disagrees with `codes`. `data.field` is `provider` or `bank`, whichever to fix |
| `400` | `bank_not_offered` | Your business doesn't open customer accounts at that bank or provider in this currency (`nuvion` always answers this for now). Leave `bank` and `provider` out, or contact support |
| `400` | — | `customer was not found`; both `customer_id` and `customer_reference` sent; BVN missing, not found or not verified; USD settlement wallet missing or short |
| `409` | `reference_in_progress` | The first request with this `reference` is still opening the account. Retry shortly |
| `409` | `customer_has_account_at_bank` | Nothing was opened. The same person already holds an account at this bank under another of your customer ids: `data.customer_id` is that customer, `data.account` the account |
| `409` | `account_limit_reached` | Nothing was opened. Either the bank can't open another account for this customer (`data.accounts` lists their accounts there), or the person already holds your business's limit of open accounts (`data.limit`, `data.active`) |
| `409` | `account_in_progress` | Another request is opening this customer's account right now. Retry shortly |
| `409` | `customer_id_conflict` | The customer's BVN was verified and `id_number` is a different one. `data.customer_id` is the customer. Use a new `customer_id` for a different person |
| `422` | — | New customer: a detail is missing or in the wrong format. `errors` lists each field |
| `422` | `invalid_customer_details` | The bank rejected the customer's details (`data.field` when it named one). Fix them before retrying |
| `422` | `nomba_identity_limit` | You sent `"bank": "nombank"` and Nombank already holds two accounts for this customer's BVN or NIN. Leave `bank` out to open at Paga, or reuse one |
| `422` | `identity_required` | No BVN or NIN for the customer. Send `id_number` |
| `503` | `issuing_unavailable` | Nombank can't be reached or didn't confirm the account, or we couldn't start opening it. No account was given to you; one the bank opened may still be confirmed later. Retry with the same `reference` (`data.retryable` is `true`); contact support if it persists |

See [Creating Accounts](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts) for the full guide.
