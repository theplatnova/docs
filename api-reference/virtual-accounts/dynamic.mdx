## Overview

Generate a single-use NGN account for a customer, for an amount and a lifespan (5 to 1440 minutes). Show the payer the account, the amount and `expires_at`.

`customer_id` can be our customer id or your own; with the customer's details in the request, a new customer is created under your id (see [Use your own customer ids](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts#use-your-own-customer-ids); on this route `customer_date_of_birth` is always required for a new customer).

## The bank

Dynamic accounts open at **Nombank MFB**: leave `bank` out or send `"bank": "nombank"`. Show the payer the `bank_name` from the response.

A Nombank dynamic account takes exactly `amount` and stops accepting payments at `expires_at`: the payer's bank refuses any other amount and any late transfer.

- Nombank opens an account only against the customer's BVN or NIN, so one must be on file (send it as `id_number` when you open the customer's persistent account). Without one: `422` `identity_required`, `data.field` `id_number`.
- At most two Nombank accounts per BVN or NIN: `409` `nomba_identity_limit`.
- Nombank can't be reached: `503` `issuing_unavailable`, retryable. Nothing was opened.
- `bank` other than `nombank`: `400` `bank_not_offered` (or `invalid_bank` for a name we don't know).

Without `reference`, a customer has one open account at a time; a repeat for the same amount returns it, and a different amount gets a new account.

## Order reference

Send your order id as `reference` (or `order_reference`) to give the order its own account. The response returns it as `order_reference`, and `account.funded` carries it as `reference`, with `expected_amount`. Repeating the request with the same reference, customer and amount while the account is open returns the same account. Any other reuse answers `400` `duplicate_reference` (`data.status` `expired` once the account has expired). Without `reference`, a customer has one open account per bank at a time: a repeat for the same amount returns it, and a different amount gets a new account (the earlier one still credits whatever is paid into it).

A payment of a different amount, or after `expires_at`, is still credited; a late one is marked `"late": true` on the webhook. See [Collecting payments per order](/virtual-accounts/collecting-per-order).
