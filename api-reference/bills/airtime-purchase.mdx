## Overview

Top up a Nigerian mobile number. Your NGN wallet is debited the amount less your airtime discount; the line receives the full amount. Send an `Idempotency-Key` and a unique `reference`. Answers `202` with `status: processing`. See [Airtime and data](/bills/airtime-data).
