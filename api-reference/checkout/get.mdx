## Overview

Retrieve a checkout by its ID: `pending` until paid, then `success` with `paid_at` and `payment_reference`. No authentication is needed. The checkout answers `404` once it is more than 7 days old, paid or not, so confirm payments with [Verify Payment](/api-reference/payments/verify).
