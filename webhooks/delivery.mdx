This page covers what happens to an event between us recording it and your endpoint acknowledging it: how we retry, when an endpoint is paused, and how to get it going again.

## Acknowledging an event

An event is **delivered** when your endpoint answers our `POST` with any **2xx** status within **25 seconds**. Anything else is a failed attempt: a non-2xx status (including a 3xx redirect, which we do not follow), a timeout, a refused connection, or a DNS or TLS error.

- **Answer quickly.** Verify the [signature](/webhooks/signatures), store or queue the event, answer `200`, and do the work afterwards.
- **Answer non-2xx when you have not accepted the event**, for example when the signature doesn't verify. A `200` is final: an event you answered 2xx is not sent again. See [Answer honestly](/webhooks/signatures#answer-honestly).

## Retries

| Your endpoint... | Attempts in total |
|---|---|
| answers with a non-2xx status | **3** (the first, plus 2 retries) |
| can't be reached (timeout, refused connection, DNS or TLS error) | **4** (the first, plus 3 retries) |

Retries are spaced out over a few minutes. If every attempt fails, the event is marked `failed`. Resend it from the dashboard (**Settings → Webhooks → the endpoint → events**): **Retry** on one event, or **Retry failed** for every failed event on the endpoint.

## Paused endpoints

Each event that exhausts its attempts counts as one failure against the endpoint. A successful delivery resets the count. When an endpoint reaches **5 failed events in a row** and **none** of its deliveries has succeeded in the last 24 hours, we **pause** it:

- Your business's developers are emailed. Before that, while an endpoint keeps failing, they get at most one warning email a day.
- While paused, new events are **recorded but not sent**. They appear in the endpoint's event history.
- **A paused endpoint stays paused until you resume it.** Fix your receiver, then set the endpoint back to active in the dashboard, or with `PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}` setting its status to `active` from a signed-in dashboard session.
- Events recorded while the endpoint was paused are **not** sent automatically when you resume. Resend them from the event history with **Retry**.

## Order and duplicates

- **Events can arrive out of order**, and a retried event can arrive after a newer one. Order by the payload's own fields (`timestamp`), not by arrival.
- **The same event can reach you twice**, for example if your server did the work but timed out before answering. Make your receiver idempotent:
  - for `checkout.paid` and `checkout.failed`, deduplicate on **`data.checkout_id` + `event`**;
  - for other events, on the event name plus the payload's own reference (`data.reference` or `data.transaction_ref`).
- [Partners](/partners/webhooks) on the version 2 envelope also get an `X-Platnova-Event-Id` header, unique per event, to deduplicate on.

## Endpoint URLs

Use an `https` URL that is reachable from the public internet, and give us the final URL: redirects are not followed.
