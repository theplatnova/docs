A refund sends money from a paid checkout back to the person who paid it. You choose how much (all of it or part of it), Platnova takes the money from your wallet and returns it to the payer the way that suits the payment method they used, and you get a webhook when it lands or fails.

## How refunds work

1. **Create a refund** with `POST /v1/checkout/{checkout_id}/refund`, giving a `reason`, an `idempotency_key` and, for a partial refund, an `amount`.
2. **We debit your wallet** in the checkout's currency and send the money back to the payer.
3. **Track it** with the [`checkout.refunded` and `checkout.refund_failed` webhooks](#webhooks), or fetch it with `GET /v1/refunds/{refund_id}`.

## Which payments can be refunded

How the money goes back depends on how the payer paid, which is the checkout's payment method (the same `id` values as [`payment_options`](/checkout/initiating-checkout#request-fields)).

| Payment method | How the refund reaches the payer |
|---|---|
| `card`, `apple_pay`, `google_pay` | Back to the original card, through the provider that took the payment. |
| `bank_transfer`, `bank_account`, `ussd`, `opay` | An NGN bank transfer to the account the payment came from. If we did not receive that account's details with the payment, you supply a `destination` account in the request. |
| `crypto`, `tag_transfer`, `mobile_money` | **Not supported yet.** |

<Warning>
Crypto, Nova Tag (`tag_transfer`) and mobile money payments cannot be refunded through the API yet. A refund request for one of these is rejected with `422 refund_method_unsupported` and nothing is debited. Refund these payers yourself for now, for example with [Send payment](/payments/sending-payments).
</Warning>

## How much you can refund

The refundable amount of a checkout is **what you were credited for the payment, minus any refunds already made** (refunds that failed do not count).

What you were credited is the net amount, after Platnova's processing fee. **The processing fee is not returned when you refund.** For example, if a payer paid NGN 10,000 and you were credited NGN 9,850 after a NGN 150 fee, you can refund up to NGN 9,850 in total.

You can refund a checkout more than once, in parts, until the refundable amount reaches zero. Leave `amount` out to refund everything that is left.

## Funding and fees

Refunds are paid from your **available wallet balance** in the checkout's currency. If the balance does not cover the refund, the request is rejected with `422 insufficient_balance` and nothing is debited.

<Note>
A payment that is still waiting in a pending settlement is not in your wallet yet. Until it settles, refunding it draws on whatever else is in your balance.
</Note>

- **Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay refunds:** your wallet is debited the refund `amount`.
- **Bank-transfer refunds** (`bank_transfer`, `bank_account`, `ussd`, `opay`): these go out through the same payout system as [Send payment](/payments/sending-payments), so the normal payout fee and the stamp duty levy are charged **on top** of the refund. The payer receives exactly `amount`, and your wallet is debited `amount` plus those charges. Bank-transfer refunds need payouts to be enabled on your business; if they are not, the request is rejected with `422 payouts_not_enabled`.

If a refund fails, everything it debited from your wallet, including any payout fee, is credited back, once.

## Idempotency

Every refund request needs an `idempotency_key`: a string you choose, up to 128 characters, unique across your business. Use something tied to the refund in your own system, such as `order-1042-refund-1`.

- Sending the **same key for the same checkout and amount** again returns the original refund with `200` and does not refund twice. If a request times out or you are not sure it arrived, retry it with the same key.
- Sending the same key with a **different checkout or amount** is rejected with `409 idempotency_key_reused`.
- A repeated key returns the original refund whatever its status, including `failed`. To try again after a failed refund, send a new key.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | The refund has been accepted and recorded, and has not yet been handed to the provider. |
| `processing` | The refund is with the card provider or the bank transfer is in flight. |
| `succeeded` | The money has been sent back to the payer. Card refunds can take a few days more to appear on the payer's statement, depending on their bank. |
| `failed` | The refund did not go through. `failure_reason` says why, and your wallet has been credited back. |

`succeeded` and `failed` are final. A refund moves `pending` → `processing` → `succeeded` or `failed`; a refund the provider refuses straight away can be `failed` in the response to the create request itself, so check `status` there as well as listening for webhooks.

## Authentication

Send your API key in the `X-API-KEY` header, as with the rest of the API. A request made with a dashboard session instead of an API key needs a team member who has the permission to send payments, and a completed two-factor check.

## Create a refund

`POST /v1/checkout/{checkout_id}/refund`

### Request fields

- **amount**: Optional. How much to refund, in the checkout's currency. Leave it out to refund the full refundable amount.
- **reason**: Required, up to 255 characters. Why you are refunding. It is for your own records and systems and is not shown to the payer.
- **idempotency_key**: Required, up to 128 characters. See [Idempotency](#idempotency).
- **destination**: Optional. An NGN bank account to send a bank-transfer refund to: `account_number` and `bank_code`. Used only for `bank_transfer`, `bank_account`, `ussd` and `opay` payments, and only when we do not have the account the payment came from. If we need it and it is missing, the request is rejected with `422 refund_destination_required`. It is ignored for card refunds.

### Example request

```bash
curl --location 'https://sandbox.api.platnova.co/v1/checkout/3e584f08-6a55-4b15-a97f-364a7d21071e/refund' \
  --header 'X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "amount": 2500,
    "reason": "Customer returned item",
    "idempotency_key": "order-1042-refund-1"
  }'
```

With a destination account, for a bank-transfer payment whose source account we do not have:

```json
{
  "amount": 2500,
  "reason": "Customer returned item",
  "idempotency_key": "order-1042-refund-1",
  "destination": {
    "account_number": "0123456789",
    "bank_code": "058"
  }
}
```

### Example response

```json
{
  "status": true,
  "data": {
    "id": "6f1c2b7e-9a4d-4c1e-8f3a-2d5b7c9e1a04",
    "checkout_id": "3e584f08-6a55-4b15-a97f-364a7d21071e",
    "payment_reference": "TRNSF_8K2M4P6Q",
    "amount": 2500.00,
    "currency": "NGN",
    "method": "bank_transfer",
    "status": "processing",
    "reason": "Customer returned item",
    "idempotency_key": "order-1042-refund-1",
    "destination": {
      "account_number": "0123456789",
      "bank_code": "058",
      "account_name": "ADA OBI"
    },
    "payout_reference": "SND_5H7J9L1N",
    "created_at": "2026-10-11T10:00:00Z",
    "updated_at": "2026-10-11T10:00:01Z",
    "completed_at": null
  },
  "error": [],
  "message": "operation was successful"
}
```

## The Refund object

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `id` | The refund's ID. Use it with `GET /v1/refunds/{refund_id}`. |
| `checkout_id` | The checkout that was refunded. |
| `payment_reference` | The transaction reference of the checkout payment being refunded. |
| `amount` | What the payer gets back, in `currency`. Fees charged to you are not included. |
| `currency` | The checkout's currency. |
| `method` | How the payer paid: the checkout's payment method `id`, such as `card` or `bank_transfer`. |
| `status` | `pending`, `processing`, `succeeded` or `failed`. See [Statuses](#statuses). |
| `reason` | The reason you gave. |
| `failure_reason` | Why the refund failed. Present only when `status` is `failed`. |
| `idempotency_key` | The key you sent. |
| `destination` | Bank-transfer refunds: the account the money was sent to, with `account_number`, `bank_code` and `account_name`. `null` for card refunds. |
| `payout_reference` | Bank-transfer refunds: the reference of the payout that carried the refund. |
| `provider_reference` | Card refunds: the card provider's reference for the refund. |
| `created_at` | When the refund was created. |
| `updated_at` | When the refund last changed. |
| `completed_at` | When the refund reached `succeeded` or `failed`; `null` until then. |

## Retrieve a refund

`GET /v1/refunds/{refund_id}` returns a single [Refund object](#the-refund-object), or `404 refund_not_found` if there is no such refund on your business.

```bash
curl --location 'https://sandbox.api.platnova.co/v1/refunds/6f1c2b7e-9a4d-4c1e-8f3a-2d5b7c9e1a04' \
  --header 'X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY'
```

## Errors

A refund request that cannot be made returns `status: false` with a machine-readable code in both `error[0]` and `data.code`. Branch on the code, not on `message`, which is written for people and may change.

```json
{
  "status": false,
  "data": {
    "code": "amount_exceeds_refundable",
    "refundable": 1500.00
  },
  "error": ["amount_exceeds_refundable"],
  "message": "The amount is more than is left to refund on this checkout"
}
```

| HTTP | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 404 | `checkout_not_found` | There is no checkout with this ID on your business. |
| 404 | `refund_not_found` | There is no refund with this ID on your business. |
| 422 | `checkout_not_paid` | The checkout exists but has no settled payment to refund. |
| 422 | `amount_exceeds_refundable` | `amount` is more than is left to refund. `data.refundable` says how much is left. |
| 422 | `nothing_to_refund` | The checkout has already been refunded in full. |
| 422 | `refund_method_unsupported` | The payment was made by crypto, Nova Tag or mobile money, which cannot be refunded through the API yet. |
| 422 | `refund_destination_required` | A bank-transfer refund needs a `destination` because we do not have the account the payment came from. |
| 422 | `insufficient_balance` | Your wallet balance in the checkout's currency does not cover the refund (and, for bank-transfer refunds, its fees). |
| 422 | `payouts_not_enabled` | Bank-transfer refunds need payouts, which are not enabled on your business. |
| 409 | `idempotency_key_reused` | This `idempotency_key` was already used for a different checkout or amount. |
| 400 | — | A field is missing or invalid. Same shape as other [validation errors](/errors#field-validation-errors). |

None of these errors debits your wallet.

## Webhooks

Two webhooks tell you how a refund ended. Subscribe to them in your [webhook settings](/webhooks/overview) like any other event.

| Event | Sent when |
|---|---|
| `checkout.refunded` | A refund reaches `succeeded`. |
| `checkout.refund_failed` | A refund reaches `failed`. Your wallet has been credited back. |

### checkout.refunded

```json
{
  "event": "checkout.refunded",
  "timestamp": "2026-10-11T10:02:13Z",
  "customer_id": "",
  "data": {
    "refund_id": "6f1c2b7e-9a4d-4c1e-8f3a-2d5b7c9e1a04",
    "checkout_id": "3e584f08-6a55-4b15-a97f-364a7d21071e",
    "payment_reference": "TRNSF_8K2M4P6Q",
    "amount": 2500.00,
    "currency": "NGN",
    "method": "bank_transfer",
    "status": "succeeded",
    "reason": "Customer returned item"
  }
}
```

### checkout.refund_failed

```json
{
  "event": "checkout.refund_failed",
  "timestamp": "2026-10-10T16:40:03Z",
  "customer_id": "",
  "data": {
    "refund_id": "4d8a2c6e-1b3f-4d5a-9c7e-6b8d0f2a4c16",
    "checkout_id": "7a1d3f5b-8c2e-4a6d-b9f1-3e5c7a9d1b48",
    "payment_reference": "TRNSF_3D5F7H9K",
    "amount": 10.00,
    "currency": "USD",
    "method": "card",
    "status": "failed",
    "reason": "Partial refund: late delivery",
    "failure_reason": "The card provider declined the refund"
  }
}
```

Use `refund_id` to match the webhook to the refund you created, and to ignore a repeated delivery of the same event.
