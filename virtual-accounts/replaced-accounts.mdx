---
title: "Replaced accounts"
slug: "virtual-accounts/replaced-accounts"
---

When a bank stops receiving payments for Platnova, each customer account you opened at that bank is given a replacement at another bank. In November 2026 this applies to **PalmPay and Paga**: every PalmPay and Paga customer account gets a **Nombank MFB** account. A PalmPay account that was already given a Paga replacement you were told about keeps it, and that Paga account in turn gets a Nombank one.

You don't need to do anything to keep receiving money. **Money sent to an old account is still credited to your customer's balance**, and you still receive `account.funded` for it. What you do need to do is give each customer their new account number before the cut-over date.

## What happens, in order

1. **A replacement is opened.** If your customer already has a Nombank account with you, that account becomes the replacement. Otherwise we open one using the BVN or NIN you gave us. You don't receive `account.issued` for a replacement.
2. **You are told.** You receive one [`account.replaced`](/webhooks/overview#account-replaced) webhook per account, with the old and new numbers and the cut-over date. Your business's Owners and Admins also get an email with the count and the date.
3. **Before the cut-over date**, the old account is still your customer's default account, and lists show it first. The replacement appears beside it with `replaces` set.
4. **From the cut-over date**, the old account is no longer listed; its replacement is the customer's default. `GET /v1/virtual-accounts/replacements` and `account.replaced` still name both. The old number keeps receiving payments, credited to your customer as before.

## Fields on a customer account

`GET /v1/virtual-accounts?customer_id=…` and `GET /v1/customers/{id}/virtual-accounts` return these fields alongside the usual ones:

| Field | On | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `replaced_by` | the old account | The `id` of its replacement |
| `replaces` | the replacement | The `id` of the account it replaces |
| `retired` | the old account, from the cut-over date | `true`: give your customer the replacement instead |
| `default` | both | `false` on a retired account |

## The full list

`GET /v1/virtual-accounts/replacements` returns every replaced account for your business: the old and new account details, `replaced_at`, `cutover_at` and `retired`. Add `?format=csv` to download it as a spreadsheet with these columns:

`customer_id, customer_reference, previous_virtual_account_id, previous_account_number, previous_bank_name, previous_bank_code, new_virtual_account_id, new_account_number, new_account_name, new_bank_name, new_bank_code, replaced_at, cutover_at`

```bash
curl https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/replacements?format=csv \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" -o replaced-accounts.csv
```

## Customers Nombank can't serve

- **Nombank already holds two accounts for the customer's BVN or NIN:** they are moved to **Paga** instead. A customer whose old account is at Paga keeps it; a PalmPay customer is given their live Paga account, or a new one, and you're told through `account.replaced` like any other replacement.
- **No BVN or NIN on file, or Nombank refused for another reason:** the customer keeps their old account, listed and receiving payments. Nothing is hidden for them. To collect from them now, use a [dynamic account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) with the customer's BVN as `id_number`.
