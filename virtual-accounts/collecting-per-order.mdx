---
title: "Collecting Payments per Order"
slug: "virtual-accounts/collecting-per-order"
---

A customer with several unpaid orders (loans, invoices, instalments) pays by bank transfer. A single account per customer tells you **who** paid, but not **which order** the money is for. Give each order its own account, bound to your order reference, and every payment notification names the order.

## The idea

A customer, `CUST-10293` in your system, has 10 unpaid orders. You open 10 accounts, one per order, each bound to your order reference. When money lands in one, the [`account.funded`](/webhooks/overview#account-funded) webhook carries that order's `reference`, so you mark exactly that order paid.

You use your own ids throughout. You never need to store ours.

## Choose the kind of account

| | Persistent account with `reference` | Dynamic account with `reference` |
|---|---|---|
| Endpoint | [`POST /v1/virtual-accounts/create`](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/create) | [`POST /v1/virtual-accounts/dynamic`](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/dynamic) |
| Lives | Until you stop using it | For `lifespan_minutes` (up to 24 hours) |
| Best for | Loans and instalments repaid over weeks | One payment per order, checkout-style |
| Amount | None set; any transfer is credited | Set per order; sent back as `expected_amount` |
| Webhook `reference` | The account's reference | The order's reference |

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Name the customer by your own id">
Send your customer id as `customer_id`. The first time, include the customer's details and the customer is created with your id as their `reference` (see [Use your own customer ids](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts#use-your-own-customer-ids)). After that, your id alone is enough. Your id is the customer: a new id creates a new customer even if another of your customers has the same email, so keep one id per person.
</Step>
<Step title="Open one account per order">
Persistent:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/create \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "CUST-10293",
    "reference": "LOAN-2026-0042",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "id_number": "22212345678",
    "customer_name": "Ada Obi",
    "customer_email": "ada@example.com",
    "customer_phone": "08030000000"
  }'
```

Or dynamic:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/dynamic \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "CUST-10293",
    "amount": 15000,
    "lifespan_minutes": 60,
    "reference": "ORDER-7781"
  }'
```

Repeat for each order with its own reference. Repeating a request for the **same** order returns the same account, so retries are safe.
</Step>
<Step title="Show each order's account to the customer">
Store the account number against the order and show it next to that order.
</Step>
<Step title="Match each payment on the webhook">
On `account.funded`, look up the order by `data.reference`. Compare `paid_amount` with what the order is owed (`expected_amount` on dynamic accounts), then mark the order paid once, keyed on `transaction_ref` so a repeated webhook can't apply twice.

```json
{
  "event": "account.funded",
  "customer_id": "6f1c2b9e-4a7d-4c3e-9b1a-2d5e8f7a6c01",
  "data": {
    "reference": "ORDER-7781",
    "customer_reference": "CUST-10293",
    "expected_amount": 15000,
    "paid_amount": 15000,
    "amount": 14950,
    "fee": 50,
    "transaction_ref": "DEP_k3J9xQ2mT8vL5nR1",
    "virtual_account_number": "9000043059"
  }
}
```

(Abridged; see [account.funded](/webhooks/overview#account-funded) for every field.)
</Step>
<Step title="Verify when in doubt">
[`POST /v1/payments/verify`](/payments/verification#customer-payments-into-virtual-accounts) with the order reference returns the payment, or `order_unpaid` if nothing has been paid into that order's account yet.
</Step>
</Steps>

## When the bank can't open more accounts

Some banks can issue only one account per customer. If the customer already holds an account at such a bank, opening another answers `409` `account_limit_reached`, and `data.accounts` lists the customer's existing accounts there. Nothing is opened. Either:

- choose another bank with `codes` on the request, or
- use dynamic accounts for the orders, which are not limited this way.

A person can also hold at most **20** open persistent accounts with your business by default, counted across every customer id that shares their BVN. One more answers `409` `account_limit_reached` with `data.limit` and `data.active`. Dynamic accounts expire and don't count; contact support if you need a higher limit.

## Things to know

- **Different amounts are credited.** A transfer that doesn't match the order is still credited (less our fee). Decide in your system what an underpayment or overpayment means for the order.
- **Several transfers into one account** are each credited, each with its own webhook.
- **Late payments are credited.** A payment into a dynamic account after `expires_at` is credited and marked `"late": true` on the webhook.
- **An account without a reference identifies the customer, not the order.** Payments into it carry `customer_reference` but no `reference`.
- **References are yours, and private to your business.** Another business using the same reference never sees your accounts or payments.
