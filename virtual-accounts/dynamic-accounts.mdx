Dynamic virtual accounts are single-use NGN bank accounts generated on demand for a customer, an amount and a lifespan. Give each one your order reference and the payment notification tells you which order was paid.

## What Are Dynamic Virtual Accounts?

When you create one, you specify:

- The **customer** it belongs to: our customer id or your own (see [Use your own customer ids](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts#use-your-own-customer-ids))
- The **amount** to collect
- The **lifespan** in minutes (5 to 1440)
- Optionally, your **order reference**

Show the payer the account details, the amount and `expires_at`.

## Key Features

### One Account per Order

Send `reference` (your order id; `order_reference` is accepted too) and the order gets its own account. The [`account.funded`](/webhooks/overview#account-funded) webhook for a payment into it carries your `reference` and the `expected_amount`, so you know which order to mark paid. See [Collecting payments per order](/virtual-accounts/collecting-per-order).

### Amount

The account is opened for the amount you set, and that is the amount to show the payer. A transfer of a different amount is **still credited** (less our fee), and several transfers into one account are each credited with their own webhook. Compare `paid_amount` with `expected_amount` on the webhook and decide what an under- or overpayment means for your order.

### Expiry

The account is meant to be paid before `expires_at`. A payment that arrives after it is **still credited**, and its webhook carries `"late": true`. Issue a new account for a new attempt rather than resending the old details.

### Instant Provisioning

Accounts are created and ready to receive payments immediately. The full bank details are returned in the response and can be displayed directly to the payer.

## Generating a Dynamic Account

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/dynamic \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "CUST-10293",
    "amount": 15000,
    "lifespan_minutes": 60,
    "reference": "ORDER-7781"
  }'
```

If none of your customers has `customer_id` yet, add the customer's details (`customer_name`, `customer_email`, `customer_phone`, `customer_date_of_birth`) and the customer is created; see [Use your own customer ids](/virtual-accounts/creating-accounts#use-your-own-customer-ids). On this route the date of birth is always required for a new customer.

## Response Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `account_name` | The name on the generated account |
| `account_no` | The bank account number to display to the payer |
| `amount` | The amount to show the payer |
| `currency` | Currency of the account (lowercase ISO code) |
| `bank_name` | Name of the bank that issued the account |
| `reference` | The account's own reference, generated by Platnova. Sent as `account_reference` on `account.funded`. Not your order reference |
| `order_reference` | Your `reference` for the order, when you sent one |
| `email` | Email address of the customer |
| `expires_at` | When the account is due to expire |
| `payment_session_id` | Session identifier for tracking (may be empty) |
| `usage` | Usage context identifier |
| `created_at` | Timestamp when the account was created |
| `customer_id` | Our id for the customer |
| `customer_reference` | Your own id for the customer, when set |

## Repeating a Request

**Without `reference`**, a customer has one open dynamic account at a time: a second request while it is open returns the same account.

**With `reference`**, each order has its own account, separate from that one:

| You send | Answer |
|---|---|
| The same reference, customer and amount, while the account is open | The same account (safe to retry) |
| The same reference with another customer or amount | `400` `duplicate_reference` |
| The same reference after its account has expired | `400` `duplicate_reference` with `data.status` `expired`. Use a new reference |
| The same reference while the first request is still opening the account | `409` `reference_in_progress`. Retry shortly |

Order references are 1–64 characters of letters, digits and `_ . : -`, unique among your business's dynamic accounts. Anything else answers `400` `invalid_reference`.

## Best Practices

- Keep `lifespan_minutes` short (10–30 minutes) for checkout use cases.
- Always display `expires_at` to the payer so they know the deadline.
- Send your order id as `reference`, and match payments on the webhook's `reference`.
- Confirm a payment with [Verify Payment](/payments/verification#customer-payments-into-virtual-accounts) using your order reference.
