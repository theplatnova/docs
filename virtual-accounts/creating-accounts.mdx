Create virtual bank accounts instantly for your customers. This guide covers the account creation process and how to retrieve account details.

## Creating a Virtual Account

Create a virtual account for a customer you already have, by our customer id or your own:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/create \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "6f1c2b9e-4a7d-4c3e-9b1a-2d5e8f7a6c01",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "id_number": "22212345678"
  }'
```

For a customer you haven't created yet, send your own id with their details in the same request: see [Use your own customer ids](#use-your-own-customer-ids).

## Account Creation Parameters

### Required Fields

- **Customer** - `customer_id`: our customer id or your own (see [Use your own customer ids](#use-your-own-customer-ids)). Or send your id as `customer_reference`.
- **Currency** - The currency for the account: `NGN` or `USD`. USD customers need an address and identity documents on file, so create them with [Create Customer](/api-reference/customers/create) first

### Optional Fields

- **Reference** - Your own reference for this account (see [One account per loan or order](#one-account-per-loan-or-order))
- **ID number** - For NGN accounts, the customer's BVN in `id_number`. In live it is required unless the customer already has a BVN on file, and it is verified before the account is opened
- **Bank** - For NGN accounts, `paga` or `nombank` (see [Choosing a Provider](#choosing-a-provider-ngn-accounts) below)
- **Codes** - The older way to choose: an array of bank codes

## Choosing a Provider (NGN Accounts)

NGN accounts open at **Paga** or **Nombank MFB**. Leave `bank` out and your business's banks are tried in order, Paga first: when Paga can't open more accounts, the account opens at Nombank in the same request, so you don't have to retry. Send `bank` to ask for one bank only.

| `bank` | Bank shown to your customer | `bank_code` |
|---|---|---|
| `paga` | Paga | `100002` |
| `nombank` | Nombank MFB | `090645` |

Always show your customer the `bank_name`, `account_number` and `account_name` from the response; don't assume the bank. Both give a 10-digit account number, and payments into both arrive on `account.funded`. `codes` (an array of bank codes) still works.

PalmPay (`100033`) no longer issues new accounts. Accounts already opened at PalmPay keep receiving payments; each is being given a Paga replacement (see [Replaced accounts](/virtual-accounts/replaced-accounts)).

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/create \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "customer_123",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "id_number": "22123456789",
    "bank": "nombank"
  }'
```

## Account Response

```json
{
  "status": true,
  "data": {
    "customer_id": "6f1c2b9e-4a7d-4c3e-9b1a-2d5e8f7a6c01",
    "customer_reference": "CUST-10293",
    "reference": "",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "email": "ada@example.com",
    "first_name": "Ada",
    "last_name": "Obi",
    "created_on": "2026-11-03T10:15:00Z",
    "amount_debited": 0,
    "accounts": [
      {
        "id": "9b2f6a10-3c4d-4e5f-8a7b-1c2d3e4f5a6b",
        "account_number": "0123456789",
        "account_name": "Ada Obi",
        "bank_name": "Example Bank",
        "bank_code": "000000",
        "reference": "LOAN-2026-0042"
      }
    ]
  },
  "error": [],
  "message": "operation was successful"
}
```

`customer_id` is our id for the customer; `customer_reference` is yours, when set. Each entry in `accounts` carries its `id` and, when you opened it with one, your `reference`. USD accounts also carry `ach_routing_number`, `rtp_routing_number`, `wire_routing_number`, `swift_code` and `bank_address`.

## Use your own customer ids

You don't have to create the customer first and store our id. Pass **your own** customer id as `customer_id` (or as `customer_reference`; send one of the two, not both). We look it up in your business: our id first, then a customer whose `reference` is that value.

If none of your customers has it yet, include the customer's details and we create the customer, with your id as their `reference`:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `customer_name`, or `customer_first_name` + `customer_last_name` | `customer_name` is split at the first space |
| `customer_email` | Required |
| `customer_phone` | Required |
| `customer_country_code` | Defaults to `NG` for NGN |
| `customer_date_of_birth` | `YYYY-MM-DD`. **Not needed for NGN when you send `id_number` (BVN)**: the date of birth is taken from the BVN record. Required otherwise |
| `customer_gender` | Optional: `male` or `female` |

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/create \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "CUST-10293",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "id_number": "22212345678",
    "customer_name": "Ada Obi",
    "customer_email": "ada@example.com",
    "customer_phone": "08030000000"
  }'
```

- The customer is created exactly as [Create Customer](/api-reference/customers/create) would create them. Repeating the request finds the same customer; it never makes a second one.
- A missing detail answers `422` listing each missing field.
- Without details, an id none of your customers has answers `400` `customer was not found`, as before.
- Your id is the customer, not their email, phone or BVN: a new id creates a new customer even when another of your customers has the same email. One person may be several of your customers (for example one per loan); keep one id per person where you can.
- If the customer's BVN was verified and a later request sends a different `id_number`, the answer is `409` `customer_id_conflict`: one id is one person.
- Your ids are 1–64 characters of letters, digits and `_ . : -`, unique within your business.

After that, your id works anywhere a customer id is accepted. See [Your own customer ids](/customers/overview#your-own-customer-ids).

## One account per loan or order

Add `reference` to open an account for one loan, order or contract. A customer can then hold several accounts, one per reference, and every payment into an account names its reference.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts/create \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_id": "CUST-10293",
    "reference": "LOAN-2026-0042",
    "currency": "NGN",
    "id_number": "22212345678"
  }'
```

- `reference` is 1–64 characters of letters, digits and `_ . : -`, unique among your business's accounts.
- **Repeating** the request with the same reference, customer and currency returns the same account. Nothing new is opened.
- The same reference with a different customer or currency answers `400` `duplicate_reference`.
- While the first request for a reference is still opening the account, a repeat answers `409` `reference_in_progress`. Retry shortly.
- Some banks can open only one account per customer. If the customer already has an account at such a bank, the answer is `409` `account_limit_reached`, and `data.accounts` lists the customer's existing accounts there (`id`, `account_number`, `account_name`, `bank_name`, `bank_code`, and `reference` when set). Nothing is opened. Use an existing account, or choose another bank with `codes`.
- Look an account up by your reference with [`GET /v1/virtual-accounts/by-reference/{reference}`](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/by-reference).

Without `reference` nothing changes: a customer has one account per bank, and a second request for the same bank answers `bank account is already added`.

To collect one payment per order rather than keep an account open, see [Collecting payments per order](/virtual-accounts/collecting-per-order).

## Account Details

Each virtual account includes complete bank account information:

### Account Information

- **Account Number** - The bank account number for receiving payments
- **Account Name** - Name associated with the account
- **Bank Name** - Name of the bank
- **Bank Code** - Bank identification code

### Routing Information (USD Accounts)

- **ACH Routing Number** - For ACH transfers
- **RTP Routing Number** - For real-time payments
- **Wire Routing Number** - For wire transfers

### International Transfers

- **SWIFT Code** - For international wire transfers

## Account Types

Virtual accounts support different account types depending on the currency:

- **Current Account** - Standard checking account
- **Savings Account** - Interest-bearing account

The account type is determined automatically based on currency and provider capabilities.

## Multiple Accounts

Customers can have multiple virtual accounts in different currencies, and several in one currency when each has its own `reference` (see [One account per loan or order](#one-account-per-loan-or-order)). Each account operates independently with its own account number and routing information.

## Listing Accounts

View all virtual accounts:

```bash
curl -X GET https://api.platnova.com/v1/virtual-accounts \
  -H "X-API-KEY: YOUR_API_KEY"
```

Filter by `currency`, and by customer with `customer_id` (our id or yours) or `customer_reference`.

## Best Practices

- Create accounts in the currencies your customers need
- Store account details securely
- Share account information with customers securely
- Monitor account activity regularly
